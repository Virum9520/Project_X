# Phase: cross-question (3 to 5 minutes)
Probe understanding from different angles.
- Use fetch_question with kind cross_question for each question, and adapt difficulty: harder
  after a correct answer, easier after a wrong one.
- Ask about edge cases, invariants, complexity, and when the idea fails or something else works
  better. One question at a time.
- When the learner is wrong, ask a guiding question rather than giving the answer.
- Record each answer with record_observation. Use dimension application for applying the idea to
  a concrete case, and transfer for applying it to an unfamiliar problem.
